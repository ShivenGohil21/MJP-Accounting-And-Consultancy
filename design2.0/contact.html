Common Questions

Everything you need to know about joining our cooperative society.

What is a housing cooperative?

expand_more

A housing cooperative is a legal entity, usually a corporation, which owns real estate consisting of one or more residential buildings. You own shares in the corporation rather than the unit itself, providing shared responsibility and community-led governance.

What is the membership application process?

expand_more

The process involves an initial inquiry, a community interview, and a financial review. We prioritize individuals who are committed to the values of shared stewardship and communal growth.

Are there units currently available?

expand_more

Availability varies across our three main sites. Please submit an inquiry form to receive our current inventory and waitlist status for specific locations.

Can I schedule a tour of the homes?

expand_more

Yes, we host monthly "Hearth Walks" for prospective members. Private tours can also be arranged for serious applicants who have cleared the initial review phase.
